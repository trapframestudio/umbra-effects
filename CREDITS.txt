  Ascii1457, as shipped modified by Painter of the Zone.

  mods when present (in GAMMA: "New mutants icons parts" by TDLemon,
  meshes\dynamics\zpf\raw_*); this mod does not ship those meshes.

  Armour feedback reads the visual tables of Wepl's Hit Effects
  (configs\plugins\wepl_hit_effect_visuals.ltx) like Painter does.

  gamedata\particles\**                         copied unchanged
  gamedata\textures\<particle texture dirs>\** copied unchanged
  gamedata\textures\wm\wm_blood_1 .. _22,
    wm_blood_1_1 .. 1_3, wm_blood_drip_*,
    wm_blood_drop_*                             MODIFIED: colour-graded
  gamedata\shaders\r3\effects_wallmark_blood.ps MODIFIED: adds the
                                                spreading-pool mode
  gamedata\scripts\zgo_tables.script            MODIFIED: from
                                                short_range_blood_lookup_tables;
                                                human arms enabled, notice added
  gamedata\configs\mod_system_zgo_engine_marks.ltx  values from
                                                mod_system_blooddecals.ltx

Everything else (all zgo_* scripts, the zgo_* wallmark, pool and drip-run
textures, the droplet particles in particles\zgo with their textures in
textures\zgo, configs and menus) is new work for this mod.


A NOTE BEFORE PUBLISHING
  Some of the particle textures inherited from Painter of the Zone appear
  to be taken from commercial games (folder names crysis_3, fallout4,
  farcry4, unrealengine). That is fine for personal use and for playing with
  friends; check them before uploading this mod anywhere public.
